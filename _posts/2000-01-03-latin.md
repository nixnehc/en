## Latin/Abbr.

1. per se
  - The f.m.p does not give decidability per se.
    
2. see $\not=$ cf.   
    Use `see` to identify the source of some information, evidence, argument, etc., not `cf.`. 
    There is a distinction between `see` and `cf.`: 
    use `cf.` only to mean “compare” or “see, by way of comparison.” 
    Neither term is italicized in notes.

3. desiderata: n. 〈拉〉迫切需要得到之物 （desideratum 的复数）

4. w.l.o.g. = Without loss of generality
5. ..., etc. / ..., and so forth.
